Compiled Oct 2026 from arkoflightforallnations.com, gospelpillars.org, and press coverage (Vanguard, ThisDay, Independent, BusinessDay, Trek Africa).

## What it is

The Ark of Light for All Nations is the **global headquarters of Gospel Pillars International Churches** (the church arm of Isaiah Wealth Ministries), located at **Plot 11C Kudirat Abiola Way, Alausa, Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria**.

- **Launched:** November 11, 2023 (dedicated by Bishop David Oyedepo as chief guest; Bishop Wesley Arije of March of Faith USA presiding father; Prophet Isaac Anto of Ghana; worship leader Ron Kenoly, Panam Percy Paul, Chioma Jesus attended)
- **Construction:** Completed in about 4 months from foundation to finishing
- **Site:** ~8,900 sqm of land in the Ikeja axis of Lagos
- **Capacity:** 10,000 worshippers across multi-roomed auditoriums
- **Cost:** Multi-billion Naira facility
- **Architecture:** Iconic arches, 100% paved floors, garden scenery for a serene worship atmosphere

## Founding vision (scripture basis)

God's promise to Dr. Isaiah Macwealth of a "city of refuge for all nations in these last days":

- "The cities which you shall give to the Levites shall be the six cities of refuge…" — **Numbers 35:6a**
- "…for mine house shall be called a house of prayer for all people" — **Isaiah 56:7**

## The three pillars

1. **Sanctuary of Refuge**
   - Mission: To host vibrant worship every week, where people from all walks of life meet to fellowship with God's word in an atmosphere of joy and faith.
   - Also: a safe haven for individuals and families escaping conflict, persecution, natural disasters and displacement.
   - Facilities: secure welcome accommodations, communal spaces, mental health services, legal aid, cultural support.
   - Programs: trauma-informed care, life-skills workshops.
   - Eligibility: open to all, regardless of nationality, ethnicity, or religion.

2. **Food Bank Storage (Ark Food Bank)**
   - Mission: To train, provide food and relief items to **20,000 less privileged families every year**.
   - Operated by OneSound Charity, an arm of The Isaiah Wealth Initiative (TIWI).
   - Serves communities in Lagos with free food, clothing, and emergency supplies.

3. **Emergency Response Centre**
   - Mission: To provide immediate assistance and support in times of crisis, natural disasters, or humanitarian emergencies.
